FAQs

Where can I get Zhong?

Zhong is out now on the Google Play Store for Android and the App Store for iPhone — search for “Zhong” by Take Care Studios LLC.

Does Zhong need an internet connection?

No. The character and vocabulary database ships inside the app, so the node explorer and every quiz mode work offline. Your progress stays on your device — see the Privacy Policy for the details.

Can I study Traditional characters instead of Simplified?

Yes. Every one of the 2,380 characters can be displayed as either Simplified or Traditional, and you can switch between them at any time.
