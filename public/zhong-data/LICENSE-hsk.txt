HSK level assignments and their English glosses (hsk1.json - hsk6.json, and
the HSK-sourced entries merged into words.json and the category files) came
from:

    https://github.com/gigacool/hanyu-shuiping-kaoshi   (MIT)

by way of a CSV reformatting of it at:

    https://github.com/plaktos/hsk_csv   (no stated license)

The HSK vocabulary lists themselves are published by the Chinese Ministry of
Education and are factual content, not the property of either project above.
The English glosses are overwhelmingly CC-CEDICT text (a comparison against a
current CC-CEDICT matches 71% of them exactly, rising to ~84% once CC-CEDICT's
own later editorial rewordings are allowed for); CC-CEDICT is credited
separately in the app and our adapted files are published under CC BY-SA 4.0.
