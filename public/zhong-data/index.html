Zhong — Open Dictionary Data

These JSON files are the dictionary data bundled in the Zhong Chinese-learning app: characters, words, radicals, HSK vocabulary lists, and themed category lists, each entry carrying simplified and traditional forms, pinyin, and English definitions.

License: the definitions are adapted from CC-CEDICT (© CC-CEDICT contributors, CC BY-SA 4.0). In accordance with that license, these adapted files are made available under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license. Changes made to the original CC-CEDICT material include: reformatting into JSON, resolving cross-references, abridging entries to at most seven senses, converting tone numbers to pinyin diacritics, and reordering by frequency. Frequency ordering draws on FrequencyWords (CC BY-SA 4.0); example sentences within entries are from Tatoeba (CC BY 2.0 FR); radical metadata derives from the Kangxi radical set and the Unihan Database (© Unicode, Inc., Unicode License).
