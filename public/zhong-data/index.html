Zhong — Open Dictionary Data

These JSON files are the dictionary data bundled in the Zhong Chinese-learning app: characters, words, radicals, HSK vocabulary lists, and themed category lists, each entry carrying simplified and traditional forms, pinyin, and English definitions — plus the phono-semantic composition table (character_components.json).

License: the definitions are adapted from CC-CEDICT (© CC-CEDICT contributors, CC BY-SA 4.0). In accordance with that license, these adapted files are made available under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license. Changes made to the original CC-CEDICT material include: reformatting into JSON, resolving cross-references, abridging entries to at most seven senses, converting tone numbers to pinyin diacritics, and reordering by frequency; the HSK files instead carry a single short gloss per entry, and their traditional-character column is hand-curated (most recently 49 forms corrected 2026-08-08).

The HSK word lists themselves come from hanyu-shuiping-kaoshi (gigacool, MIT), by way of hsk_csv (plaktos); the MIT notice is reproduced in LICENSE-hsk.txt, which accompanies these downloads. The phono-semantic composition table (character_components.json) is adapted from Wiktionary (English and Chinese editions, CC BY-SA 4.0); changes made: the semantic/phonetic component pairs were extracted from the wikis' glyph-origin templates (only a hand-verified subset of the Chinese edition, whose etymologies describe the traditional forms), each component was tagged with the script it applies to, and standard decompositions were hand-authored for characters neither wiki covers. Frequency ordering draws on FrequencyWords (CC BY-SA 4.0) — the app's quiz level orderings (levels.json) are derived from it; example sentences within entries are from Tatoeba (CC BY 2.0 FR); radical metadata derives from the Kangxi radical set and the Unihan Database (© Unicode, Inc., Unicode License).
